# Research notes

This platform is an undergraduate research prototype.

Research outputs must not be interpreted as clinically validated until appropriate evaluation, ethical approval, institutional approval and clinical validation have been completed.

Legitimate remaining research dependencies (not software defects):

- Approved paediatric dataset and ethics clearance
- Final label schema and WHO-linked anthropometric calculators
- Clinically validated stagnation / deterioration / follow-up thresholds
- Live Component 2 explainability, Component 3 counterfactuals, Component 4 drift services
- Hospital FHIR connectivity
